◈ SITUATION ASSESSMENT

Hun Manet's government is holding macro stability — Pornmoniroth's 6.6% growth projection remains defensible — but the coherence between economic ministries and the political centre is deteriorating as deadline pressure mounts. The structural inheritance from Hun Sen's patronage architecture is becoming operationally costly at precisely the moment Cambodia needs clean institutional signalling to Brussels and Washington.

The result: MEF is doing serious fiscal planning for a revenue baseline that the political centre is making increasingly unlikely to materialise. This produces a hidden fiscal cliff that will not appear in any official budget document until it is too late to adjust. Policy coherence is a fiction at the macro level until this contradiction is resolved.

◈ SITUATION ASSESSMENT

Cambodia enters the final quarter of 2026 in a structurally precarious position: the 2029 EBA deadline is now close enough to impose real investment uncertainty on garment buyers, while the Funan Techo Canal construction continues to deepen Chinese infrastructure dependency at precisely the moment the government needs to signal Western-facing credibility. Macro fundamentals remain stable by Pornmoniroth's fiscal-first standards — revenue mobilisation is tracking and the riel is holding — but the growth model itself is under compression from three simultaneous external shocks: EU market access conditionality, US tariff pressure on exports, and regional manufacturing competition from Vietnam and Bangladesh. Hun Manet's multi-vector foreign policy positioning is being tested at the seams, and the government's capacity to execute coherent reform across these fronts is constrained by the patronage architecture inherited from his father.

⚠ CONFLICT ALERT

The single most dangerous internal contradiction is the structural collision between **Say Sam Al's extraction-permitting environmental ministry and Sun Chanthol's FDI attraction mandate at CDC**. Chanthol's pitch to Western and Japanese manufacturers increasingly depends on ESG credibility — sustainable supply chains, deforestation-free sourcing, clean energy zones. Say Sam Al's ministry, operating on a short-horizon patronage logic with no operational environmental compliance mechanism, is actively degrading the very credibility signals Chanthol needs to close deals. This is not a policy disagreement that can be resolved in an inter-ministerial meeting — it reflects incompatible time horizons and incompatible political economies (CPP land-developer patronage networks versus FDI attraction). The practical consequence is that Cambodia's SEZ expansion story is vulnerable to NGO and investigative journalism exposure of deforestation concessions approved by the same government promoting green manufacturing, which gives Western buyers a ready-made exit justification at the worst possible moment. Expect no resolution; expect management through compartmentalisation and communications suppression.

The Sri Lanka case predicts Cambodia does not crisis in 2026 — it crises in 2029–2031, when the EBA termination, canal debt service, and garment sector disruption coincide.

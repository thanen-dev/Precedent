◈ SITUATION ASSESSMENT

⚠ CONFLICT ALERT

The most dangerous internal contradiction is between **Say Sam Al's extractive concession regime and Cham Nimul's EBA survival strategy**. The EU's EBA conditions include environmental governance benchmarks alongside labour and democratic governance criteria. Say Sam Al's ministry continues to approve concessions — deforestation, land clearance, resource extraction — that generate direct evidentiary ammunition for EU reviewers assessing Cambodia's compliance trajectory. Nimul cannot credibly defend Cambodia's EBA position in Geneva or Brussels while her cabinet colleague is running an approval process that produces the documentation EU officials will cite against her. This is not a theoretical tension: it is a live policy incoherence with a 30-month deadline. Neither official has the bureaucratic authority to constrain the other, and Hun Manet has shown no appetite to adjudicate between patronage-linked ministries and technocratic priorities. If this contradiction is not resolved at the Prime Minister level, it will surface as a Brussels finding in the next EBA monitoring report and narrow Cambodia's already-constrained negotiating room.

The parallel predicts Cambodia will find itself unable to use ASEAN institutions as a hedge against Chinese pressure at exactly the moment it needs that hedge most.
